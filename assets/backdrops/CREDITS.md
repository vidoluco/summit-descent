Painted backdrops generated with Google Gemini in October 2026 for this project. Each one repaints a
real photo from Wikimedia Commons in the game's style, so the place is recognisable. The style reference is the first
Dolomiti backdrop, kept with the other prompt-only versions in v1-prompt-only/. The reference photos are kept locally in
data/raw/backdrop-photos/ (not committed). There are two files per world: `<id>.jpg` is the view looking downhill along
the course, and `<id>-summit.jpg` is the mountain itself as people photograph it. A world can add a per-stage view
when the course turns (K2 stage 3 uses `k2-glacier.jpg`).
